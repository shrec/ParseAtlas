# Derivability audit

**ქართული ვერსია:** [derivability-audit.ka.md](derivability-audit.ka.md)

**Written:** 2026-09-05. Covers ROADMAP.md's Working Theses and the reports of experiments 0001 through 0052.

## The admission this document opens with

**No prior-art search has ever been done for this project, and this audit is not one.** Nobody here has queried a bibliographic database or read a survey. Three places in the repository put a result of this project beside a published one, and not one of them is a search. `docs/grammar-space-structure.md` placed `4` Generation 1 structural results — R2, R3, R4 and R5, carried into ROADMAP.md as T7, T8, T9 and T11 — against named prior art, and concluded that one is a restatement of a classical data structure, one a textbook construction, one trivial by counting and one a tautology. 0051 carries a *PRIOR ART.* block that puts the published defining constants of the reference ellipsoid beside the ones its own module derives, in a comparison table, and files the evidence line "prior art, stated from knowledge and checked". 0044 attributes the register-numbering rule behind `peak_live` to a published result by author, title, journal and year, and says in the same sentence that it is "cited here as the classical result it is, not as anything this project invented". All three state their prior art from knowledge. Not one of them consulted a database.

README.md and docs/README.md — not ROADMAP.md — promise that "several striking-looking results are deliberately marked there as folklore, tautology, or trivial-by-counting, with the prior art named", and the *there* they point at is ROADMAP.md's Working Theses section. The word folklore occurs `2` times in README.md, `1` time in docs/README.md and `0` times in ROADMAP.md, so of the `3` marks the promise names, the one that never appears in the file being promised about is the one whose word the reports do not use. The other two, and restatement beside them, are applied with prior art named to the Direction A results of 0019 through 0025.

**The reports do mark their own non-novelties, and this audit is not the first place that happens.** Of the `19` Direction B reports, 0034 through 0052, `7` mark one of their own results as not new, or argue in their own terms about whether it is:

- 0041 files its chain result as "a **null model result**, not a discovery", and again in its evidence classification as "explicitly not a finding about conventions".
- 0046 files its `4` `by_construction` invariants as "**symbolically justified**, and reported as restatements of the parser's shape rather than as findings", and says of one of them that it "is a restatement of how parsing works and is worth nothing as a discovery".
- 0049 argues in its own terms about whether its headline is a tautology, and settles it: "the observation is not a tautology, because `G0` chooses among an object's *declared* writings while the space also holds every other parenthesization".
- 0051 writes that "the three height writings are classical and none of them is new here; what is new is the observation of what they cost each other".
- 0052 writes that "the generalisation is classical and is what Pratt parsing already uses".
- 0044 classifies its depth and peak-memory numbers as "**classical result plus computation**, cited ... and not claimed as new".
- 0036 keeps the accuracy result of its far zone with the note that "the reason is trivial and that is exactly why it is worth recording".

Beyond those `7`, every one of the `19` marks something of its own as forced, by construction, a rule consequence, a null-model artefact, a choice rather than a theorem, or a withdrawal. The frame was declared, and it was filled far more often than a reader of ROADMAP.md alone would find.

So the premise of this audit is narrower than "the marks are missing", and it is this. There is **no systematic search of the literature**: nobody has queried a database, and the three places above state their prior art from knowledge. There is **no single place where the marks are collected**, so a reader has to find each one inside the report that makes it, in whatever section and whatever words that report chose. And **most Direction B results carry no mark at all**: the marks land on named subsets, so a result can sit unmarked in a report that marks something else — 0041 marks its chain result and not its parallelism tax, 0046 marks `4` of its `16` quantities and not its characterisation, 0051 marks its `3` height writings and not its decode obligation. This audit is the collection and the classification. It is still not a literature search.

**This audit contains not a single citation. Not an author, not a year, not a title.** The reason is that this worker has no reliable literature access, and a fabricated reference inside a novelty assessment is worse than no assessment at all. Naming a field and a set of search terms is a hint for someone with a database; naming a paper that may not exist is a lie with a footnote. Where a search term is an eponym the reports themselves already use — a technique named after the people who published it — it stands there as the word a database will match, not as a reference: no work, no year and no title appears anywhere below.

So the question asked here is one the repository can answer about itself:

> For each load-bearing claim: does it follow in one paragraph from the definitions this project already states?

A result that follows in one paragraph from stated definitions is not a discovery, whether or not a paper exists for it. That judgement needs no library. Where the answer is no — where the claim genuinely required the measurement — this document says so, and names the field and the search terms that would find prior art, so that a human with database access can check it in an afternoon.

**This document establishes non-novelty where it can, and never novelty.** The strongest statement it permits itself about any claim is that the claim is contingent and would need a literature check. Not a single row below asserts that anything here is new.

## The verdicts

- **DERIVABLE.** Given this project's own definitions, a competent reader reaches this claim in one paragraph. The paragraph is written out in the third column. Where a paragraph could not be written, the verdict is not DERIVABLE.
- **MEASURED.** The claim is contingent on the corpus, the registry or the machine, and could have come out otherwise. These are the only rows where novelty is even a question. Where part of the claim is forced, the forced part is written in the third column as a remark, and the verdict still follows the contingent part.
- **METHODOLOGICAL.** The claim is a fact about how this project works, not about mathematics. Valuable internally; not a contribution.
- **UNCLASSIFIED.** The claim could not be classified, and the reason is given. An honest UNCLASSIFIED row is worth more than a guess.

Four reading rules, because the verdicts are easy to misread:

- **MEASURED does not mean hard, large, or new.** It means contingent. Contingency is a necessary condition for novelty and nowhere near a sufficient one. An exhaustive enumeration of a declared finite registry is MEASURED — it is contingent on the registry — and is still only a count.
- **DERIVABLE does not mean worthless.** A derivable claim that was verified over millions of cells has rigour, and rigour is a real property of work. It is not a discovery.
- **Where ROADMAP.md already marks a thesis as a restatement, a tautology or trivial by counting, this audit records the mark and does not re-litigate it.** T7, T9 and T11 are in that position.
- **Experiments 0053 and 0054 are in flight as this is written and are excluded.** Not a single claim below comes from either.

The first group is textbook restated; the second is a set of numbers about this registry and this corpus.
- **0038's trade is qualitatively obvious, and the null model is not what rescues it.** The judgement is half right. That a sum shows coefficients and a product shows roots is DERIVABLE and 0038 says so: the two shapes are mutually exclusive at the root of the tree, and 0038 files `2` of its `8` exclusions as rule consequences for exactly that reason. What the null model establishes is only that the ranking is not carried by objects that cannot move, which is hygiene rather than content. **The measured content of 0038 is elsewhere and it is sharper than the trade:** `constant_term` and `roots` are never manifest together in `850` cells although the rules permit it — a writing of the `a * x` kind would satisfy both and simply never wins — and `factor_structure` is never manifest in `5` named worlds including our own. Those could have come out otherwise. The trade could not. The headline that no world shows everything is MEASURED for the same reason: the root split forces it only for the objects that straddle it, and which objects those are is corpus.
- **0049's `13` frontier points are MEASURED, and 0048's refinement direction is MEASURED as well.** Both halves are confirmed. 0049 is confirmed twice over: the `13` writings that no world produces naturally are contingent on `4` declared axes, a reference world, a hand-chosen corpus and 0038's rules, and the frontier headline itself turns out to be contingent in the same way, because its premise — that G0's own writing is the unique bracket minimum — holds in G0 and fails in all `24` other reference worlds. On 0048 the earlier edition of this audit was wrong. It argued the excluded-set half of the class key and disposed of the canonical-form half in a sentence; but the canonical-form half is not a project definition, it is 0048's own **observation** of `0` of `300` cells and the report explicitly calls it "a property of this operator set rather than of domains in general". A derivation that needs an observation to close is not a derivation. What `42112` sources measure is both the **direction** and the **rate** — `1` of `12`, `316` of `40000`, and the `51` of `57` excluded-set differences that never reach the partition.

## What this project has that is not a claim

Everything above is a proposition with a truth value. What follows is not, and it is the larger part of what has been built.

- **A corpus with a verified equivalence oracle.** Every writing in the Direction B corpora is checked to denote the object it claims to: `667` candidate writings survive an exact-rational probe at declared points and all `667` are confirmed symbolically; round trips are witnessed forwards rather than inferred from an absence of failures, `16675` in 0049 and `1250` in 0038 and 0046; and every bracket a rendering emits is deleted in turn and shown to change the tree, `88171` deletions in 0049 and `1811` in 0043. A corpus without that is a list of strings.
- **Conservative extensions proved between generations.** When the language grew an operator, the new registry was shown to reproduce the old parser cell by cell before a single new number was read, and the same discipline is now required of any further extension. The proof is derivable from T6; the audit is what makes it a fact about the code rather than about the mathematics.
- **Minimality established by exhaustive search rather than assertion.** The invariant writing of 0046 is not asserted minimal; each of its `147` bracket pairs is deleted and a world that then reads a different tree is named. The saturating bound of 0052 is not declared; bounds past it were run and the parser count did not move.
- **Null models that separate registry artifacts from findings.** T6 supplies the baseline, and it is applied rather than mentioned: every rate in 0046 and 0048 is computed inside an operator-support stratum, 0034 and 0037 recompute their rankings on the movable subcorpus, 0038 removes the objects with a constant manifest set, and 0024's whole negative result exists because the excess matrix was taken against the support-forced baseline. Without it a rising class count across a widening registry is the registry widening and nothing else.
- **A review discipline that returns cards for the conclusion and not the code.** In the last wave `5` of `8` cards were returned, and the defects were in what the report claimed rather than in what the module computed: an inflated count of distinct objects, a frontier question answered narrower than it was asked, an enumeration bound read as a property of the rules, a splitting mechanism attributed to the wrong axis, and a containment argued as a theorem where it is a measurement. The rule that came out of that wave — every decisive predicate carries a negative control that puts a case it must reject in front of it, through the same path the published number takes — was written because three cards in a row shipped a gate no test could have caught failing.

**Is an apparatus a contribution, or only a precondition for one?** Stated plainly: **only a precondition, with one qualification.**

The apparatus is what makes the claims above checkable, and it is why this audit could be written at all — the reason a verdict of DERIVABLE could be reached for so many rows is that the reports state their definitions, their regimes and their limits precisely enough for a derivation to be attempted against them. That is a real property and most work does not have it. But nobody outside this project has a reason to care that a corpus of `34` hand-chosen objects has a verified oracle. An apparatus becomes a contribution only when it produces a result somebody else would want, or when it is packaged so that somebody else can run their own question through it, and neither of those has happened here.

The qualification is the review discipline. A record of `5` cards returned in one wave, with the defect named in the conclusion rather than the code, and a rule extracted from three consecutive failures of one and the same shape, is a finding about how this kind of work goes wrong — and it is stated in a form that transfers. Whether it is new is exactly the question this document is not equipped to answer.

## Limits of this audit

- **It classifies claims, not their importance.** A DERIVABLE verdict says the claim follows from stated definitions; it says nothing about whether the definitions were the right ones to state, and choosing them is most of the work in several of these cards.
- **The derivations were written by reading the reports, not by re-running the modules.** Where a derivation depends on a definition, that definition is the one the report states; if a module diverges from its report, the derivation inherits the divergence. It is also why a row whose derivation would close only with a fresh computation is MEASURED rather than DERIVABLE: that computation was not run here.
- **A DERIVABLE verdict is not a proof of non-novelty.** A claim can follow in one paragraph and still be unstated in the literature, and the paragraph being short is not evidence that anyone wrote it down. What the verdict rules out is the claim being a discovery for the person who states it.
- **A MEASURED verdict is not evidence of novelty.** It is the weaker statement that the claim is contingent, which is where a literature check would have to start. Most MEASURED rows here will have prior art; this document cannot say which.
- **Experiments 0001 through 0033 are covered through their theses rather than row by row.** Their results are consolidated in ROADMAP.md's Groups A through D and in `docs/grammar-space-structure.md`, and this audit classifies them at that granularity. Every thesis in the file now carries a row, so no experiment in that range is silently absent; what is absent is a per-experiment reading of them.
- **Experiments 0053 and 0054 were in flight and are excluded**, so the table is complete through 0052 and no further, and T33 is unclassified for that reason.
