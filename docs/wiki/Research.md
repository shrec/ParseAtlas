## Evidence route

Use this page as a compact map, not as a replacement for the reports. Start with the [documentation and experiment index](../README.md). For the preserved claim register, read the [original claim register](../derivability-audit.md). For literature judgments, read the [full research and prior-art audit](../novelty-audit-2026-09-05.en.md).

The audit scope is `111` claims through `0052`, plus separate `0053/0054` supplements and `35` sources; `0056` is not included. Evidence labels describe how a claim is supported inside the repository. They are not novelty labels. A derivation follows from definitions and explicit assumptions; it is different from a measurement, a model, or a hypothesis. A bounded literature search can compare against selected sources, but it cannot certify novelty.
